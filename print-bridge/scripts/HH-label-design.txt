Houseplant Hospital — Label design (with Hilda logo) on Mini
===========================================================

MacBook preview is updated with the real Hilda gold wordmark.

To print on the Mini you must copy BOTH:
  - print-bridge/src/label.ts
  - print-bridge/assets/   (whole folder — contains the logo)


----------------------------------------
STEP 1 — On MacBook: copy these onto the Mini
----------------------------------------

From:

  /Users/jackchalkley/Documents/Cursor Projects/Hilda_Houseplant-Hospital/print-bridge/src/label.ts
  /Users/jackchalkley/Documents/Cursor Projects/Hilda_Houseplant-Hospital/print-bridge/assets/

Onto Mini:

  ~/Hilda/Houseplant-Hospital/print-bridge/src/label.ts
  ~/Hilda/Houseplant-Hospital/print-bridge/assets/


----------------------------------------
STEP 2 — On Mini: confirm logo file exists
----------------------------------------

ls ~/Hilda/Houseplant-Hospital/print-bridge/assets/hilda-logo-gold-800.png


----------------------------------------
STEP 3 — Restart bridge
----------------------------------------

bash ~/Hilda/Houseplant-Hospital/print-bridge/scripts/install-launchd.sh

curl -s http://127.0.0.1:8787/health
